## Additional adversarial finding

The report left multisig claim release ordering as an unresolved hypothesis.
Tracing that path found one further Low defect: confirm, revoke, execute,
propose, deploy, and class-declare catch blocks released their durable claim for
*every* thrown submission error. `IndeterminateRpcWriteError` means the write
may already be live, so releasing its hashless claim reopened the blind retry
the ledger exists to prevent.

The shared failure-release helper now retains claims only for indeterminate
writes and releases them for determinate pre-broadcast or node rejection. A
regression drives the real message path twice: after the first ambiguous
broadcast, the second action is refused and only one invoke reaches the RPC
stub.

Adversarial review of the first declaration-fee patch found a second Low defect:
an undeployed-account card displayed a declaration-plus-deployment total but
echoed only the declaration component. A fee refresh on another wallet surface
could therefore leave the first surface authorizing a total it never displayed.
The click now echoes the exact combined total, the background recomputes it from
its current components before claiming the request, and retry invalidates the
old total synchronously. Each component still reaches its own signing boundary
as a separate ceiling (`MCW-PR234-002`).

## Unresolved-hypothesis review

- Multisig proposal ids intentionally mirror the pinned OpenZeppelin component
  and omit chain id. The watched contract and local records are network-scoped,
  and the account transaction signature binds the Starknet chain, so no
  cross-chain signing replay was found.
- `waitForTransaction` is finite in pinned starknet.js 10.0.2. Privacy passes
  explicit retry and interval ceilings; account deployment inherits the pinned
  provider's finite retry count and the wallet's per-request transport timeout.
  The report's "unbounded" hypothesis was not confirmed.
- The starknet.js broadcast interception seam is now checked against the actual
  pinned `RpcProvider` channel, in addition to behavioral transport-loss tests.
  A dependency change that removes `channel.fetchEndpoint` or `channel.invoke`
  fails locally before release.
- `apps/terminal` remains a separate web application and trust perimeter, as
  the report stated. Its transport review remains in the audit sequence rather
  than being silently represented as completed extension work.

## Adversarial controls

The remediation tests were fault-injected one guard at a time. Removing the
required RPC-result hash guard failed invoke, declare, and deployment tests;
removing swap invalidation reopened the stale confirm card; allowing a missing
declaration fee consumed the held request and failed the retry test; releasing
an indeterminate multisig claim admitted a second broadcast; disabling stale
accepted-claim release wedged re-confirmation; and bypassing the proposal claim
made the bounded duplicate-proposal test fail. The workflow policy suite also
mutates the Chromatic token back to job scope and rejects it. Removing the
combined declaration-total echo or its synchronous retry invalidation fails the
two-surface deployment-fee regression.

Final release-gate and exact-head CI evidence is recorded in the PR handoff for
the commit containing this follow-up.
